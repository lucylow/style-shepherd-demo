### Voiceover Tips

- **Pace**: Clear and steady, ~150 words per minute
- **Energy**: Enthusiastic but professional
- **Pauses**: Brief pauses at key moments for visuals to register
- **Emphasis**: Stress "autonomous", "multi-agent", "28% reduction"

### Transitions

- Use smooth fades between scenes (0.5s fade)
- Quick cuts for impact moments
- Zoom/pan effects on key UI elements

### Music/Sound

- Light, upbeat background music (optional)
- Subtle sound effects for agent interactions (optional)
- Ensure narration is always clear and audible
